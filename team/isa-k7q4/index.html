ISA · Call script

Call script

Double dial every call Quick hang-up → call a 3rd time Never give a price Calls 8am–8pm their time Call from the client's number

Goal: a day, a time, and every decision-maker there

Short and friendly. Talk like a neighbor.
01
OpenWho you are and why

Hey Maria, it's Uriel with Valencia. You asked about a free estimate for your bathroom. Got a quick second?

02
Learn the jobOne question at a time
What are you looking to get done?Full bathroom or just the shower?Tile, slab or acrylic?Getting a few quotes?When do you want it done?
03
Set the visitAlways give two choices

The owner, Brian, comes out, measures, and brings samples. It's free. Does Thursday at 10 or Friday at 2 work better?

Then get the address.

04
Spouse / decision-makerEveryone at the visit

Will your wife or anyone else who helps decide be there too? We bring samples and different options, so you can all pick together. That way nothing changes later and everyone's happy with the quote.

05
Lock it inRepeat it back

Perfect. Thursday at 10 at 123 Oak St. Brian, the owner, will be there. I'll text you so you have it.

06
After the call
  • Book it on the client's calendar
  • Stage → Booked - Confirmed
  • Post it in the client's group chat

Clicks are in CRM steps.

No prices. Not even "around." The owner gives the price at the visit.

Short answer, then back to the day and time

Runs automatically

  • New lead: texts go out and you get call tasks, 8am–8pm.
  • Booked online: call tasks + texts until they answer.
  • Stops when they text YES or you set Booked - Confirmed.
  • Reminders before the visit send by themselves.

Your job

  • Work the call list in Manual Actions.
  • Book on the right calendar.
  • Keep the stage right.
  • Post new visits in the client chat.
  • Mistake? Tell Leo.

Login to confirmed

Click a screenshot to enlarge
01

Open Manual Actions

Your call list. Work it top to bottom.

Careful: "Let's Start" dials right away. If "Call Anyway?" shows up late at night, press Cancel.
02

Call from the client's number

In the dialer, click Change next to "Calling From" and pick the number from Clients.

Never pick a number marked OLD SPAM.
03

Book the visit

On the contact: Appointments → Book.

Check: it opens on the wrong calendar. Pick the client's calendar and time zone.
04

Set the stage

Opportunities → edit → Stage.

Talked, they said yesBooked - Confirmed
Not reached yetBooked
Not interestedDead
05

Tags

  • appt-confirmed is added for you when you set Booked - Confirmed.
  • Never add lead nurturing to someone with a visit booked.
  • Wrong tag? Click the x to remove it, then tell Leo.
06

Send it to the client

Post in the client's group chat (Sendblue, inside the CRM). Use the contact's Lead Profile to fill this in.

Here's a new appointment:
• Name: Maria Lopez
• Phone: (209) 555-0142
• Address: 123 Oak St, Modesto
• Project: Full bathroom, tub to walk-in shower, tile
• Estimate Appointment: Thu Nov 5, 10:00 AM
• Notes: Husband will be there. Wants it done before the holidays.
07

Answer texts

Conversations shows every reply. Answer fast and short.
