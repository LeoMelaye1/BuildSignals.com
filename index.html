FAQ

Quick answers

What do I actually pay?

A one-time $2,500 setup fee. After that, nothing until you close a job from an appointment we booked. When you close, we get an agreed percentage of that job. We set the percentage for your market on the call.

What's the $2,500 for?

Getting you launched: we script your ads, shoot and edit the videos, build the campaigns and lead forms, and set up the calling and booking. It's paid once, not monthly. The setup fee goes up as our client roster fills.

What do you do after launch?

Everything between the ad and your calendar. We run and adjust the ads, call every lead within 3 minutes, qualify them, book the appointment on your calendar, and confirm it with every decision-maker the day before.

And what do I do?

Show up to the appointment and close the job. That's it.

How do you know when I close?

Closed jobs are logged in the live dashboard you get access to, where you also see every lead and appointment we book for you.

Who is this for?

Bathroom remodelers doing $1M or more a year, with crew capacity for more jobs and an owner or sales rep who runs in-home appointments. Anywhere in the United States. We take on 5 new clients a month.
