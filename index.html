How the free 3 work

Your first 3 sat appointments are completely free. We cover the ad spend, build and run everything, call every lead within 3 minutes, and confirm the appointments. After 3, you decide. Walk away and you owe us nothing.

What counts as a sat appointment

A homeowner, not a renter. Every decision-maker present. Budget confirmed before we book it. And the appointment actually happened in their home. If it doesn't meet all four, it doesn't count as a sat appointment.

Step 3: keep going or stop

No long-term contract. Stay on per appointment or per lead for as long as it's working. Per close means no upfront fee and we're paid a percentage of the jobs you close. It's a program you have to qualify for, and it's offered to select clients only.

How billing works

FAQ

Questions contractors ask before they apply

How much does it cost?

Your first 3 sat appointments are completely free, ad spend included. After that you pick how you pay: per sat appointment (bought in blocks of 10), per lead, or per close. We set the numbers for your market once your area qualifies. No setup fee, no long-term contract.

What counts as a sat appointment?

A homeowner (not a renter), with every decision-maker present, who confirmed a budget for the project, and the appointment actually happened in their home. If it doesn't meet all four, it doesn't count as a sat appointment.

How do the first 3 free appointments work?

They're free, full stop. We cover the ad spend, build and run the campaigns, call every lead within 3 minutes, and confirm the appointments. Once you've sat 3, you decide whether to continue and how you want to pay. If you walk away, you owe us nothing.

Who pays for the ads?

For your first 3 appointments, we do. After that it depends on the plan you pick, and we lay it out in plain numbers once your area qualifies.

What's the difference between per appointment, per lead, and per close?

Per sat appointment: you pay for each appointment that meets the sat definition, bought 10 at a time. Per lead: you pay for every qualified lead that comes in and your team books them. Per close: no upfront fee, we're paid a percentage of the jobs you close. Per close is a program you have to qualify for.

Who qualifies for pay per close?

Select clients only. Contractors who close jobs from their first appointments, have the crew capacity to take on more work, and report closed jobs through our dashboard. Everyone else stays on per appointment or per lead.

Who is this for?

Bathroom remodelers only, doing $1M or more a year, with two or more crews and an owner or sales rep who runs in-home appointments. We work with bathroom remodelers anywhere in the United States and take on 5 new clients a month.

Do you work with kitchen remodelers, roofers, or general contractors?

No. We work exclusively with bathroom remodelers, including full bathroom remodels, tub-to-shower conversions, and walk-in tub and shower installers. If bath is a side service rather than your core business, we're not the right fit.

What does "see if your area qualifies" mean?

You tell us your service area and a few numbers about your business. We check that we can run bath campaigns in your market and that you're a fit. If yes, we set up your free 3. No sales call required to find out.

How quickly do appointments start?

Ads go live within 7 days of onboarding. Every lead is called by our team within 3 minutes of coming in, and first sat appointments usually land inside the first two weeks.
