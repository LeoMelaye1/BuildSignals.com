Questions contractors ask

What does it cost?

$1 puts your card on file. For your first 30 days there is no retainer: you pay $100 per shown appointment, charged once a week. After day 30 it is $1,000 a month plus $100 per shown appointment. Your ad budget goes straight to Meta from your own ad account, with a $75 a day minimum.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. If it stops working for you, you stop.

How fast do you call new leads?

Every new lead gets a call in under 5 minutes. We book the appointment on your calendar and confirm it before you drive out.

Do you work in my area?

We work with contractors and home improvement companies across the US, one company per trade in each area. Book a call and we'll check yours.

What trades do you work with?

Every home improvement trade: bathrooms, kitchens, roofing, full remodels and more. If your reps sell in the home, we can book for them.

Who is behind BuildSignals?

Leo Melaye founded BuildSignals in 2016. We're based in Davis, California, and our clients have closed over $2.8M in remodeling jobs. Meet the team on our About page.
