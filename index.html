FAQ

Questions bath companies ask first

How much does a sat bathroom appointment cost?

$297, with the ad spend included. You open a balance of $5,940, which covers your first 20 sits, and each appointment that sits comes off it. There's no retainer and no contract.

What counts as a sit?

Your rep in the home with every decision-maker. No-shows, cancellations, reschedules, one-legs, appointments you choose not to run and homeowners already in your CRM in the last 90 days don't come off your balance.

Who calls the homeowners?

Our setters, in under 5 minutes, on recorded lines. They screen against your criteria, book onto your reps' calendars and confirm before the appointment. Every lead and recording goes into your CRM.

What should I expect from 20 sits?

That depends on your close rate and job size, which are yours. At 20 to 30 percent on a $15,000 bath job, 20 sits is 4 to 6 jobs and $60,000 to $90,000. If your first 20 sits sign less than $59,400 within 90 days, the 10x guarantee adds 10 sits free.

Do you book kitchens, roofing or windows?

Not right now. We only book bathroom appointments: full remodels, tub-to-shower conversions, walk-in showers and tubs, and bath replacements.

Can we stop?

Any time, with 7 days' notice. Sits you haven't used are refunded.

Do we need a CRM?

If you have one, we connect to it for free. If you don't, we build you one for $1,500, so every lead, call and outcome is tracked.

Who is this for?

Bathroom remodelers with 3 or more people running in-home appointments and 40 or more demos a month. One bath company per area.
