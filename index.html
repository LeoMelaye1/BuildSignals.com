Questions remodelers ask

What does it cost?

You don't pay us anything for your first 2 weeks. After that you choose: pay per sat appointment, at a price set for your market on our call, or pay per close, as an agreed percentage of the jobs you close.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. If it stops working for you, you stop.

How fast do you call new leads?

Every new lead gets a call in under 5 minutes. We book the appointment on your calendar and confirm it before you drive out.

Do you work in my area?

We work with bathroom remodelers across the US, one remodeler per area. Book a call and we'll check yours.

Who is behind BuildSignals?

Leo Melaye founded BuildSignals in 2016. We're based in Davis, California, and our clients have closed over $2.8M in remodeling jobs.
