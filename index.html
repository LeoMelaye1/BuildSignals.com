FAQ

Quick answers

What does "your first 3 are on us" mean?

We book your first 3 sat, budget-confirmed appointments at no cost to you, ad spend included. We run the ads, call every lead within 3 minutes, and confirm the appointments. After 3, you decide. Walk away and you owe us nothing.

What counts as a sat appointment?

A homeowner (not a renter), with every decision-maker present, who confirmed a budget for the project, and the appointment actually happened in their home.

How do I pay after the first 3?

Per lead, per sat appointment (bought in blocks of 10), or per close. We set the numbers for your market once your area qualifies. No setup fee, no long-term contract.

Who qualifies for pay per close?

Select clients only. Per close means no upfront fee and we're paid a percentage of the jobs you close. It's for contractors who close jobs from their first appointments, have the crew capacity to take on more work, and report closed jobs through our dashboard.

Who is this for?

Bathroom remodelers only, doing $1M or more a year, with two or more crews and an owner or sales rep who runs in-home appointments. Anywhere in the United States. We take on 5 new clients a month. No kitchens, roofing, windows, or general remodeling.

How fast do appointments start?

Ads go live within 7 days. Every lead is called within 3 minutes of coming in, and first sat appointments usually land inside the first two weeks.
