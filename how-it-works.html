Frequently asked questions

What does BuildSignals do?

BuildSignals is a lead generation and appointment-setting agency for residential kitchen and bath remodelers in the United States. We build and run your Facebook and Instagram ads, call and qualify every lead, and book pre-qualified, budget-confirmed appointments on your calendar.

How does the free 2-week trial work?

You try BuildSignals free for 2 weeks. We set up your ads and start booking appointments. After the trial you decide whether to keep going and how to pay: per appointment or per close. No card is required to set up the trial.

How does pay per appointment work?

You pay a set price for each qualified appointment we book for you. Your card is charged once a week for the appointments delivered that week. The price depends on your market and is set on a 20-minute call. No long-term contract.

How does pay per close work?

Instead of paying per appointment, you pay an agreed percentage of the jobs you close from appointments we booked. If an appointment doesn't turn into a sold job, you don't pay for it.

Which model should I pick?

Pay per appointment suits contractors who close well and want a simple, predictable weekly cost. Pay per close suits contractors who would rather only pay when work is sold. You can talk it through with Leo on the call.

What counts as a qualified appointment?

A homeowner who is interested in a remodel, has confirmed a budget, and has booked a time for an in-home estimate. We confirm the appointment before you go out.

Who pays for the ads?

You pay Meta directly for ad spend from your own ad account, so you own the account and see every dollar. We build, run, and optimize the campaigns inside it.

Do I need a long-term contract?

No. There are no long-term contracts with either model.

Who is BuildSignals for?

Residential kitchen and bath remodeling contractors anywhere in the United States who want a steady flow of booked in-home estimates.
