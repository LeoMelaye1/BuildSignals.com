What we do

BuildSignals runs Facebook ads for companies that remodel homes. Most of them do bathrooms and kitchens. A homeowner sees an ad and fills out a form to ask for a free estimate. That's where your job starts.

Your job in one picture

Every new lead goes through the same five steps. You take a homeowner who asked for a price and turn them into a visit the owner knows about.

Call script

Every call has one goal: get a day, a time, and everyone who decides at the visit. Keep it short and friendly.

Before every call

  • Always double dial. No answer? Call right back.
  • If they pick up and hang up fast, call a third time.
  • Call from the client's number.
  • Only call between 8am and 8pm their time.
  • No exact price on the phone. You can say "most projects start at $8,000."
  • Ready to book? Stop asking questions and book.
  • New lead, no answer on the double dial? Leave a voicemail (below).

Voicemail

Calling a new lead for the first time and nobody picks up? Leave this voicemail on your second call.

"Hey Maria, you requested an estimate for your bathroom 2 days ago. This is [your name] with Valencia. We'll try you again later. Thanks."

Use their name, their project, how long ago they asked (say "today" if it was today), and the client's name.

Must do on every call

Do these 4 steps in order. If they're ready to book at any point, stop asking questions and book.

Say hi

Who you are and why you're calling

"Hey Maria, it's [your name] with Valencia. You requested an estimate for your bathroom?"

They say: "Yes, I remember."

"Great. What were you looking to get done exactly?"

Use their project, like bathroom or kitchen. You can say estimate or quote.

Materials, then who decides

Ask these two right after each other

"Any idea on materials? Tile, slab, acrylic? We bring samples of all the options so you can see them."
"And since we'll be going over all these options, we want to keep it productive. Is there anyone else who helps decide, like a wife or husband, who should be there too?"

Ask about materials first. Then asking who else decides sounds helpful, not pushy.

Set the visit

Always offer two times

"The owner, Brian, comes out, measures, and brings the samples. It's free. Does Thursday at 10 or Friday at 2 work better for both of you?"

Only offer times the client can come. Check their days on Clients and the open times on their calendar. Then get the address.

Say it back

Repeat the day, time and address

"Perfect. Thursday at 10 at 123 Oak St. Brian, the owner, will be there. I'll text you so you have it."

Extra questions (optional)

Use these if they want to talk or aren't sure yet. Skip them if they're ready to book. Pick one or two.

  • "Full bathroom, or just the shower?"
  • "When are you hoping to get it done?"
  • "Are you getting a few quotes?"
  • "What made you want to do it now?"

After the call

  1. Book it on the client's calendar. See Book a visit.
  2. Check the Lead Profile and take out any price. See CRM steps.
  3. Move the stage to Booked - Confirmed. About a minute later, the owner gets the Lead Profile by text.
  4. Tell the owner in their group chat. See Sendblue.

Confirm a visit

Some leads book a visit online by themselves. Nobody has talked to them yet, so we don't know if it's real. Your job is to call, make sure they're coming, and change the stage from Booked to Booked - Confirmed.

How to spot them

  • Your call list says Self-booked: confirm.
  • Their stage says Booked.
  • They have the tag appt-unconfirmed.
  • Their stage isn't Booked? Change it to Booked right away. Everyone who books a visit must be on Booked.
  • You booked them yourself on the phone? Skip this page and go right to Booked - Confirmed.

Steps

1. Look before you call

Open the contact. Find these:

  • The day and time of the visit, on the right side.
  • What they asked for on the form.
  • The city. Is it in the client's area? Check Clients.

2. Call them

Call from the client's number. Double dial. They hang up fast? Call a 3rd time.

3. What to say

They already said yes by booking, so don't sell. Just get ready for the visit.

Listen to a real one:

Say why you're calling:

"Hey Maria, it's [your name] with Valencia. Saw you booked for Thursday at 10. Got a quick second so we're ready when we come out?"

Ask what they want:

"What were you looking to get done exactly?"

Ask about materials, then who decides:

"Any idea on materials? Tile, slab, acrylic? We bring samples of all the options so you can see them."
"And since we'll be going over all these options, we want to keep it productive. Is there anyone else who helps decide, like a wife or husband, who should be there too?"

Say it back:

"Perfect. Thursday at 10 at 123 Oak St. Brian, the owner, will be there."

4. Check the Lead Profile and leave a note

A few minutes after the call, the Lead Profile on the contact fills in by itself. Read it and fix anything wrong before the next step. The owner gets this exact text. The steps are on CRM steps.

Take out any price you told them. If you talked about money, give a budget range.

5. Change the stage to Booked - Confirmed

  1. On the contact, click Actions.
  2. Click the three dots on the opportunity, then View Opportunity details.
  3. Click Stage and pick Booked - Confirmed.
  4. Click Update.

That's it. In about a minute, the CRM stops the confirm calls and texts and sends the owner the Lead Profile by itself. You don't touch the tags.

6. Tell the owner

Post it in the client's group chat. Start the message with Confirmed appointment:. See Sendblue. Now you're done.

The owner always gets the appointment, even if the lead never answers you. If you couldn't reach them, post it with New appointment (UNCONFIRMED): at the top.

Not every client has a Sendblue chat. The table at the bottom of Sendblue shows where each owner gets their messages.

Texting

Your job is calling. The workflows already text leads for us, so don't text unless you have a reason. When you do text, write like a normal person: one or two short lines and one question.

Only text when there's a reason

No reason on this list? Don't text. Call instead.

  • They asked you to text them.
  • They texted you first.
  • You were talking and they dropped off, and now you can't reach them.
  • You have another real reason, like you need one thing from them to get the visit ready.

Never send a text just to check in or because they didn't answer. The workflow already does that.

CRM steps

The CRM sends texts and reminders by itself. Your part is calling, booking, checking the Lead Profile, and setting the stage.

1. Open Manual Actions

Click Conversations, then Manual Actions. This is your call list. Call new leads first, then self-booked leads, then reschedules, then everyone else.

2. Work every call this one way

It's more clicks, but you see who you're calling and you call from the right number.

  1. Click the lead's name in the list. Their contact opens.
  2. Read who they are: what they asked for, their tags, and past calls and texts.
  3. Open the dialer and pick the client's number. See step 3.
  4. Double dial. They pick up and hang up fast? Call a 3rd time. Leave a voicemail if it's a brand new lead.
  5. Talked to them? Finish the call and set the stage first. See steps 4 to 6.
  6. Go back to Manual Actions. Click the trash can on that lead's row and confirm.

That deletes only the call on your list. If they didn't answer, the workflow moves on to its next step by itself. Don't text them. The workflow does that too.

Don't use "Let's Start." It dials right away, before you've seen who they are.

Only use the trash can in Manual Actions. The trash can next to their name on the contact page deletes the whole contact. Never click that one.

If a box asks "Call Anyway?" because it's late for them, press Cancel.

3. Call from the client's number

Open the dialer. Click Change next to "Calling From" and pick the client's number. The numbers are on the Clients page.

Never use a number marked OLD SPAM or CLIENT TEXTS ONLY.

4. Book the visit

On the contact, click the calendar icon, then Add Appointment. Every step is on the Book a visit page.

The box opens on the wrong client. Every time, pick the client's calendar, the client's name as team member, and the time zone.

5. Check the Lead Profile

On the contact, click All fields and search Lead Profile. It fills in by itself a few minutes after a call. Still empty, or you booked by text? Write it yourself.

The owner gets this exact text when you set Booked - Confirmed, so fix it first:

  • Name, Phone and Address stay at the top.
  • What they want done, in their words.
  • Who will be there and the best way to reach them.
  • Plain text. Keep it short.

Never write a price you told them. Give a budget range instead, like "Budget: around $18K to $28K."

What the CRM does by itself

  • Texts every new lead right away, and keeps texting until they answer.
  • Adds calls to your list: new leads, nurture, self-booked, and follow-up days.
  • Texts the homeowner "you're all set" when you book, then reminders before the visit.
  • Texts the owner the Lead Profile about a minute after you set Booked - Confirmed. If nobody reached a self-booked lead, the owner gets it 18 hours before the visit, marked UNCONFIRMED.
  • Texts most owners their schedule every evening (tomorrow's visits) and every morning (today's). You never need to remind an owner.
  • After the visit, checks in with the owner and texts the homeowner. That's the client's part.

Book a visit

Two things must be right every time: the client's calendar and the client's name as team member. Always book while you're logged in as you. That's how your bookings are counted.

1. Open the booking box

On the lead's contact, click the calendar icon on the right, then Add Appointment.

2. Pick the client's calendar

The box usually opens on the wrong client. Click Calendar, type the client's name, and click their calendar.

Not sure which client? Look at the lead's tags. Calendar names are on the Clients page.

If you pick the wrong calendar, the lead gets texts from the wrong company and the wrong client gets the visit.

3. Pick the client's name as team member

Click Team member and pick the client's name, like Watkinson Construction. Each client has its own name in this list. It keeps that client's visits from landing on top of each other.

Don't leave it on Calendar Default. You don't pick your own name: the CRM already knows you booked it.

4. Set the time and book

Check the time zone is the client's. Pick the date, then a time from the Slot list, then click Book appointment.

The Slot list only shows open times, spaced so the owner is never double-booked. The lead wants a time that isn't in the list, but it's on the client's days? Ask Leo before you book it.

The homeowner gets a "you're all set" text right away by itself.

5. Check it

The visit shows on the right side of the contact. Check the client's calendar and the client's name on it. If one is wrong, click the three dots, then Edit, and fix it.

Then check the Lead Profile, set the stage, and tell the owner. See CRM steps.

Never book these on the calendar

  • A second visit. The owner is going back to measure again or show a design. Booking it would text the homeowner and the owner like it's a brand new visit. Tell Leo the day and time instead.
  • A CaliFirst lead. CaliFirst calls its own leads.
  • A Punch List visit that isn't in Driive yet. Book it in Driive first. See Clients.

Rescheduling

Someone wants to move or cancel their visit. Try to get a new day and time before you hang up.

They want to move it, and you're on the phone

On the phone

Offer two new times

"No problem at all. I have Saturday at 2 or Monday at 10. Which is better?"

If they want to cancel: "Want me to check back in a couple weeks?"

In the CRM

Three dots, then Edit

On the contact, click the three dots on the visit, then Edit. Change the date and time, check the time zone, and click Save.

The homeowner gets a new "you're all set" text by itself. That's normal. If they still get reminders for the old time, tell Leo.

Cancel the appointment

Three dots, then Cancel

On the contact, click the three dots on the visit, then Cancel.

Stop the reminders

Actions, then Workflows

Under Active workflows, click the x next to Appointment Scheduled. If you skip this, they still get "see you tomorrow" texts. Some clients have a longer name that starts with Appointment Scheduled. Remove that one the same way.

Only do this when the visit is cancelled and they have no other visit booked. Marking someone for Follow Up who is still booked? Leave it on, because they still need their reminders.

Set the follow-up

On the contact

On the contact, open the opportunity. Set the Stage to Follow Up and click Update.

Then click All fields, search Follow Up, click Follow Up Date, pick the day, and save.

Use the day they said. If they didn't say, use tomorrow. Leave a note with the time, like "Cancelled Thu. Asked about same time tomorrow."

Follow up

When they said, or by phone

They said when: reach out then.

They didn't answer your text: just call them.

They say yes to a new time: book it, set the stage back to Booked - Confirmed, clear the Follow Up Date, and tell the owner the new day and time.

Sendblue

Sendblue is the app we use to text the owners of our clients, like Brian or Eric. Each client has one group chat. That's where we tell them about their visits.

Easy way to remember

  • Homeowners (leads): call and text them in the CRM.
  • Owners (clients): text them in Sendblue.
  • Never text a homeowner from Sendblue.
  • Don't start new chats. If a chat is missing, tell Leo.

Steps

1. Log in

Go to dashboard.sendblue.com and log in with your email. Can't get in? Ask Leo.

2. Turn on your notifications

Do this one time. Then you'll know when an owner texts.

  1. Click your profile icon at the bottom left.
  2. Click Notification Settings.
  3. Under "Which leads?" pick All leads.
  4. On Inbound Message, check Desktop and Mobile.
  5. Get the Sendblue app on your phone and log in. Mobile doesn't work until you do.
  6. If your browser asks to allow notifications, click Allow.
  7. Press Test next to Inbound Message, then Done.

Didn't get the test on your computer and your phone? Tell Leo.

3. Find the client's chat

Every chat has the client's name on it. Search the name, then click the chat.

A blue dot means there's a new message.

4. Send a new appointment

Do this every time you book a visit.

  1. Copy the format and fill it in from the contact in the CRM.
  2. Pick the first line:
    • New appointment: you booked it, or confirmed it on the call.
    • New appointment (UNCONFIRMED): nobody has reached them yet.
    • Confirmed appointment: you just confirmed someone the owner already knows about.
  3. Get the address from All fields on the contact. Check there before you ever write "no address."
  4. Open the client's chat. Check the name at the top.
  5. Paste it in the message box and press Send.

Never write a price you told the homeowner. If you talked about money, give a budget range, like "Budget: around $18K to $28K."

New appointment:
Name: Maria Lopez
Phone: (209) 555-0142
Address: 123 Oak St, Modesto, CA 95350
Estimate Appointment: Thursday, Nov 19 at 10:00 AM
Project: Full bathroom. Wants the tub out and a walk-in shower with tile.
Notes: Confirmed by phone with our team. Her husband will be there.

Example lead.

5. Tell them when something changes

Send one short message in the same chat.

The visit moved:

Update: Maria moved to Sat Nov 21 at 2 PM.

The homeowner cancelled:

Update: Maria cancelled Thu Nov 19 at 10 AM. I'm following up to rebook.

6. Answer when an owner texts

Answer fast and keep it short. Click the chat, type, and press Send.

Don't know the answer? Ask Leo. Don't guess.
