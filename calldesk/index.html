On the call

Call script

The goal of every call is a day, a time, and every decision-maker at the visit. Keep it short and friendly.

Before every call

  • Every call is a double dial. No answer, call right back.
  • If they pick up and hang up fast, call a third time.
  • Call from the client's number.
  • Only call between 8am and 8pm their time.
  • No exact price on the phone. You can say "most projects start at $8,000."
  • Ready to book? Stop asking and book.

Must do on every call

4 steps

This is the whole job: a day, a time, and everyone who decides at the visit. If they're ready to book at any point, stop asking questions and book.

Open

Who you are, then one question

"Hey Maria, it's Uriel with Valencia. You asked about a free estimate for your bathroom. Got a quick second? What are you looking to get done?"

Materials, then who decides

Ask these two back to back

"Any idea on materials? Tile, slab, acrylic? We bring samples of all the options so you can see them."
"And since we'll be going over all these options, we want to keep it productive. Is there anyone else who helps decide, like a wife or husband, who should be there too?"

The materials question is the setup. Once they picture the options, asking who else decides sounds helpful, not pushy.

Set the visit

Always offer two times

"The owner, Brian, comes out, measures, and brings the samples. It's free. Does Thursday at 10 or Friday at 2 work better for both of you?"

Only offer days the client can come (see Clients). Then get the address.

Confirm

Repeat it back

"Perfect. Thursday at 10 at 123 Oak St. Brian, the owner, will be there. I'll text you so you have it."

Only if you have time

Optional

Use these when they're chatty or not sure yet. Skip them if they're ready to book. Pick one or two, not all.

  • "Full bathroom, or just the shower?"
  • "When are you hoping to get it done?"
  • "Are you getting a few quotes?"
  • "What made you want to do it now?"

After the call

  1. Book it on the client's calendar.
  2. Move the stage to Booked - Confirmed.
  3. Post it in the client's group chat.

Not booked yet

Goal: get put through. Don't explain the whole reason you called. Say only your name and the appointment.

"Hi, it's Uriel, calling about your appointment."

That's it. Wait for them to pick up. If it hangs up or goes to voicemail, treat it like no answer.

Price shopper

Goal: get a real day and time.

Not ready yet

Months away, or saving up

Goal: keep the door open.

"Totally fine. When do you think you'd want to start? I'll check back with you then."

In the CRM: move the stage to Follow Up, set the Follow Up Date, and leave a note. Moving to Follow Up stops all our texts and calls on its own. Don't add lead nurturing. Forgot the date? The CRM gives you a call task in 14 days anyway.

Follow-up day is here

Task from Follow Up Reminders

Goal: call like you promised. Read the note first.

"Hey Maria, it's Uriel with Valencia. You said today was a good day to talk about the bathroom. Still a good time?"

Then pick up the script where you left off.

Follow-up day, no answer

You called on the follow-up day and they didn't pick up

Goal: hand them back to the texts and calls. Double dial first, then send one short text:

"Hey Maria, it's Uriel with Valencia. Checking in on the bathroom like we talked about. Is texting easier?"

In the CRM: mark the call task done in Manual Actions. That's it. About 10 minutes later the CRM moves them back to Connecting, adds lead nurturing, and they get about a week of our texts and calls again.

If you reached them: book them, or set a new Follow Up Date if they want a call later. Do it within 10 minutes of marking the task done and they won't go back into the texts.

Want them back in the texts right away? Add lead nurturing yourself. The CRM clears the date and moves them to Connecting.

Already booked

Booked online, not confirmed

Task from Self-booked: confirm. Tag appt-unconfirmed

Goal: confirm the visit. Don't re-sell it.

"Hey Maria, it's Uriel with Valencia. Saw you booked for Thursday at 10. Got a quick second so we're ready when we come out?"

Ask what they want done, materials, and who decides. Repeat the day and time.

In the CRM: move the stage to Booked - Confirmed.

Wants a new time

Booked, needs to move it

Offer two new times right away. Follow the Rescheduling steps.

Cancels by text

Booked, texts to cancel

Goal: get a new time or a day to follow up.

"Thanks for letting me know, Maria. Would the same time tomorrow work?"

Only ask if the client can come then and they didn't already say when. Follow the Rescheduling steps for canceling by text.

Missed the visit

No-show

Goal: rebook the same day. No guilt.

"Hey Maria, it's Uriel with Valencia. Looks like we missed each other today. I have Saturday at 2 or Monday at 10. Which is better?"

Then move the appointment like a reschedule.

Stop calling

Not a real lead

Wrong number, didn't fill out a form, not interested, outside the area

"Sorry to bother you. Have a good day."

In the CRM: move the stage to Dead and leave a note why. Dead takes them out of all calls and texts on its own.

Lead assigned to Leo

Owner or Assigned To says Leo Melaye

Don't call or text. Skip the task and move on.

In the CRM

CRM

Texts, call tasks and reminders run on their own. They stop when the lead texts YES or you move them to Booked - Confirmed. Your part is the calls, the calendar and the stage.

1. Open Manual Actions

Conversations → Manual Actions is your call list. Work it from the top.

"Let's Start" dials right away. If a box asks "Call Anyway?" late at night, press Cancel.

2. Call from the client's number

In the dialer, click Change next to "Calling From" and pick the client's number from the Clients page.

Never use a number marked OLD SPAM.

3. Book the visit

On the contact, open Appointments and click Book.

The calendar box opens on the wrong client. Pick the client's calendar and time zone every time.

4. Set the stage

Opportunities → edit → Stage.

  • Talked and they said yes: Booked - Confirmed
  • Not reached yet: leave on Booked
  • Not interested: Dead

5. Tags

  • appt-confirmed is added for you when you set Booked - Confirmed.
  • Never add lead nurturing to someone with a visit booked.
  • Added the wrong tag? Click the x on it to remove it and let Leo know.

6. Send it to the client

Post it in the client's group chat (Sendblue, inside the CRM). Use the Lead Profile on the contact to fill it in.

Here's a new appointment:
• Name: Maria Lopez
• Phone: (209) 555-0142
• Address: 123 Oak St, Modesto
• Project: Full bathroom, tub to walk-in shower, tile
• Estimate Appointment: Thu Nov 5, 10:00 AM
• Notes: Husband will be there. Wants it done before the holidays.

Example lead.

7. Answer texts

Every reply shows up in Conversations. Answer fast and keep it short.

Schedule a follow-up

On the phone

Get a real day and time

"No problem. What day is good for me to call you back? Morning or afternoon?"

Set the date

On the contact

Open the contact, click All fields, find Follow Up Date, pick the day, and save.

Leave a note

Write the time they gave you and what they want, like "Call Tue after 3. Wants a walk-in shower."

On that day

A call task shows up in Manual Actions under Follow Up Reminders. Call them at the time they asked for.

Leads assigned to Leo

If the contact's Owner or the task's Assigned To says Leo Melaye, that's Leo's lead. Don't call or text them. Skip the task and move on.

Example call

They cancel by text

1. Text them back

Right away

They didn't say when: ask about the same time tomorrow, but only if the client can come then. Check the Clients page.

"Thanks for letting me know, Maria. Would the same time tomorrow work?"

The client can't come then: ask about the next time they can come.

They said when to reach back out: don't ask about tomorrow. Go with what they said.

"Thanks for letting me know, Maria. I'll reach back out Tuesday."

They asked for a new time: don't ask about tomorrow. Book the time they want and move the appointment the same way as on a call.

2. Tell the client

In their group chat in Sendblue

Update: Maria cancelled Thu Nov 5 at 10 AM. I'm following up to rebook.

3. Cancel the appointment

Appointment menu → Cancel

4. Set the follow-up

On the contact

Move the stage to Follow Up. Then click All fields, find Follow Up Date, pick the day, and save.

Use the day they said. If they didn't say, use tomorrow. Leave a note with the time, like "Cancelled Thu. Asked about same time tomorrow."

5. Follow up

They said when: follow up with them then.

No response: just call them.

They say yes to a new time: book it, clear the Follow Up Date, and tell the client the new day and time.
