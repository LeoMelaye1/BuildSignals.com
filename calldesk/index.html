What we do

BuildSignals runs Facebook ads for companies that remodel homes. Most of them do bathrooms. A homeowner sees an ad and fills out a form to ask for a free estimate. That's where your job starts.

Your job, in 4 steps

Call them fast

New leads always come first

When a homeowner fills out the form, call them within 5 minutes. Whoever calls first usually gets the job.

Find out what they want

Ask a few easy questions. The Call script tells you exactly what to say.

Book a free visit

This is the big one

Get a day, a time, and everyone who decides. The owner of the company goes to the home, measures, shows samples, and gives the price. You never have to know the price.

Tell the owner

Send the visit details to the owner in Sendblue.

Who is who

LeadThe homeowner who filled out the form. You call them.
ClientThe remodeling company we work for, like Valencia or Dream Bath.
OwnerThe person who runs the client's company, like Brian at Valencia. The owner goes to the home.
YouOn every call and text, you work for the client. Never say BuildSignals.

If a homeowner sees "BuildSignals" on their phone and asks, say: "That's just our phone system. I'm with Valencia."

Rules that never change

  • Say you're with the client. Never say BuildSignals.
  • Call from the client's number.
  • Only call and text from the CRM, never your own phone.
  • Only call between 8am and 8pm their time.
  • No exact prices. You can say "most projects start at $8,000."
  • Only offer days the client can come.
  • If someone says stop calling or texting, stop. Move them to Dead and turn on DND on their contact.
  • Not sure? Ask Leo. Asking is always better than guessing.

On the call

Call script

Every call has one goal: get a day, a time, and everyone who decides at the visit. Keep it short and friendly.

Before every call

  • Always double dial. No answer? Call right back.
  • If they pick up and hang up fast, call a third time.
  • Call from the client's number.
  • Only call between 8am and 8pm their time.
  • No exact price on the phone. You can say "most projects start at $8,000."
  • Ready to book? Stop asking questions and book.
  • New lead, no answer on the double dial? Leave a voicemail (below).

Voicemail

Calling a new lead for the first time and nobody picks up? Leave this voicemail on your second call.

"Hey Maria, you requested an estimate for your bathroom 2 days ago. This is [your name] with Valencia. We'll try you again later. Thanks."

Use their name, their project, how long ago they asked (say "today" if it was today), and the client's name.

Must do on every call

4 steps

Do these 4 steps in order. If they're ready to book at any point, stop asking questions and book.

Say hi

Who you are, then one question

"Hey Maria, it's [your name] with Valencia. You asked about a free estimate for your bathroom. Got a quick second? What are you looking to get done?"

Materials, then who decides

Ask these two right after each other

"Any idea on materials? Tile, slab, acrylic? We bring samples of all the options so you can see them."
"And since we'll be going over all these options, we want to keep it productive. Is there anyone else who helps decide, like a wife or husband, who should be there too?"

Ask about materials first. Then asking who else decides sounds helpful, not pushy.

Set the visit

Always offer two times

"The owner, Brian, comes out, measures, and brings the samples. It's free. Does Thursday at 10 or Friday at 2 work better for both of you?"

Only offer days the client can come (see Clients). Then get the address.

Say it back

Repeat the day, time and address

"Perfect. Thursday at 10 at 123 Oak St. Brian, the owner, will be there. I'll text you so you have it."

Extra questions

Optional

Use these if they want to talk or aren't sure yet. Skip them if they're ready to book. Pick one or two.

  • "Full bathroom, or just the shower?"
  • "When are you hoping to get it done?"
  • "Are you getting a few quotes?"
  • "What made you want to do it now?"

After the call

  1. Book it on the client's calendar.
  2. Move the stage to Booked - Confirmed.
  3. Post it in the client's group chat.

The steps are on the CRM steps page.

In the CRM

CRM steps

The CRM sends texts and reminders by itself. They stop when the lead texts YES or you set the stage to Booked - Confirmed. Your part is calling, booking, and setting the stage.

1. Open Manual Actions

Click Conversations, then Manual Actions. This is your call list. Start at the top.

"Let's Start" calls right away. If a box asks "Call Anyway?" because it's late for them, press Cancel.

2. Call from the client's number

Open the dialer. Click Change next to "Calling From" and pick the client's number. The numbers are on the Clients page.

Never use a number marked OLD SPAM.

3. Book the visit

On the contact, click the calendar icon, then Add Appointment. Every step is on the Book a visit page.

The box opens on the wrong client. Every time, pick the client's calendar, your name, and the time zone.

4. Set the stage

The stage tells everyone where the lead is. On the contact, click Actions, the three dots, then View Opportunity details. Pick the Stage and click Update.

  • You talked and they said yes: Booked - Confirmed
  • Booked, but you haven't reached them: leave it on Booked
  • Not interested: Dead

5. Tags

  • A tag is a label on a contact. Most get added for you, like appt-confirmed.
  • Never add lead nurturing to someone who has a visit booked.
  • Added the wrong tag? Click the x on it and tell Leo.

6. Tell the owner

Send the visit details in the client's group chat in Sendblue. The Lead Profile on the contact has what you need.

In the CRM

Book a visit

Two things must be right every time: the client's calendar and your name. Leo counts your bookings by your name, so this is how you get paid.

1. Open the booking box

On the lead's contact, click the calendar icon on the right, then Add Appointment.

2. Pick the client's calendar

The box usually opens on the wrong client. Click Calendar, type the client's name, and click their calendar.

Not sure which client? Look at the lead's tags. Calendar names are on the Clients page.

If you pick the wrong calendar, the lead gets texts from the wrong company and the wrong client gets the visit.

3. Put your name on it

Click Team member and pick your own name. Never leave it on Calendar Default.

Don't see your name? Book it anyway and tell Leo right after. The visit still counts for you.

4. Set the time and book

Check the time zone is the client's. Pick the date and time, then click Book appointment.

Only offer times the client can come. See the Clients page.

5. Check it

The visit shows on the right side of the contact. Look for your name and the client's calendar. If one is wrong, click the three dots, then Edit, and fix it.

Then set the stage and tell the owner. See CRM steps.

Texting clients

Sendblue

Sendblue is the app we use to text our clients. A client is the owner of the company we book for, like Brian or Don. Each client has one group chat. That's where we tell them about their appointments.

Easy way to remember

  • Homeowners (leads): call and text them in the CRM.
  • Owners (clients): text them in Sendblue.
  • Never text a homeowner from Sendblue.
  • Don't start new chats. If a chat is missing, tell Leo.

1. Log in

Go to dashboard.sendblue.com and log in with your email. Can't get in? Ask Leo.

2. Turn on your notifications

Do this one time. Then you'll know when a client texts.

  1. Click your profile icon at the bottom left.
  2. Click Notification Settings.
  3. Under "Which leads?" pick All leads.
  4. On Inbound Message, check Desktop and Mobile.
  5. Get the Sendblue app on your phone and log in. Mobile doesn't work until you do.
  6. If your browser asks to allow notifications, click Allow.
  7. Press Test next to Inbound Message, then Done.

Didn't get the test on your computer and your phone? Tell Leo.

3. Find the client's chat

Every chat has the client's name on it. Search the name, then click the chat.

A blue dot means there's a new message.

4. Send a new appointment

Do this every time you book a visit.

  1. Copy the format and fill it in. The Lead Profile on the contact in the CRM has the details.
  2. Open the client's chat. Check the name at the top.
  3. Paste it in the message box and press Send.

Never write a price you told the homeowner. If you talked about money, give a budget range, like "Budget: around $18K to $28K."
