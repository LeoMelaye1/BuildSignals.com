What we do

BuildSignals runs Facebook ads for companies that remodel homes. Most of them do bathrooms and kitchens. A homeowner sees an ad and fills out a form to ask for a free estimate. That's where your job starts.

Our clients pay us for every visit we book, so booked visits are what matter most. Their jobs start around $10,000, so we're looking for real projects, not small repairs.

Rules that never change

  • Say you're with the client. Never say BuildSignals.
  • Read the lead's info before you talk to them.
  • Always double dial. No answer? Call right back.
  • They pick up and hang up fast? Call a 3rd time.
  • They asked us to call by filling out a form. This isn't cold calling, so don't feel bad about the double dial.
  • Call from the client's number.
  • Only call and text from the CRM, never your own phone number.
  • Every call is recorded.
  • Don't text leads unless there's a reason. The workflow texts them for us. See Texting.
  • Booking by text is the last resort. Try to get them on a call.
  • Only call between 8am and 8pm their time.
  • No exact prices. You can say "most projects start at $8,000."
  • Never write a price you told them in the Lead Profile or the group chat.
  • Only offer days the client can come.
  • If someone says stop calling or texting, stop. Move them to Dead and turn on DND on their contact.
  • Not sure? Ask Leo. Asking is always better than guessing.

Call script

Every call has one goal: get a day, a time, and everyone who decides at the visit. Keep it short and friendly.

This is a starting point. If you've done sales before, use your own words. Just keep the same goal.

Before every call

  • Always double dial. No answer? Call right back.
  • If they pick up and hang up fast, call a third time.
  • Call from the client's number.
  • Only call between 8am and 8pm their time.
  • No exact price on the phone. You can say "most projects start at $8,000."
  • If they want to talk, let them talk. Getting to know them books more visits than rushing.
  • Ready to book? Stop asking questions and book.
  • New lead, no answer on the double dial? Leave a voicemail (below).

Voicemail

Calling a new lead for the first time and nobody picks up? Leave this voicemail on your second call.

"Hey Maria, you requested an estimate for your bathroom 2 days ago. This is [your name] with Valencia. We'll try you again later. Thanks."

Use their name, their project, how long ago they asked (say "today" if it was today), and the client's name.

Must do on every call

Do these 4 steps in order. If they're ready to book at any point, stop asking questions and book.

Say hi

Who you are and why you're calling

"Hey Maria, it's [your name] with Valencia. You requested an estimate for your bathroom?"

They say: "Yes, I remember."

"Great. What were you looking to get done exactly?"

Use their project, like bathroom or kitchen. You can say estimate or quote. If they're short with you, stay warm and ask again gently. You can't book a visit until you know what they want.

Materials, then who decides

Ask these right after each other

"Any idea on materials? Tile, slab, acrylic? We bring samples of all the options so you can see them."

They're not sure?

"We have three main options: acrylic, stone slabs and tile. They're all at different prices. Do you have an idea where you'd like to fall?"

Most people rule out at least one. Then:

"Great. I'll leave a note so Brian brings samples of those, so we can show you the options and you can decide."
"And since we'll be going over all these options, we want to keep it productive. Is there anyone else who helps decide, like a wife or husband, who should be there too?"

The samples are your reason for asking who else decides. That way it sounds helpful, not pushy. Never just ask "Is your wife going to be there?" People get defensive when they feel sold to. Owners care about this more than anything: both homeowners at the visit.

Set the visit

Always offer two times

"The owner, Brian, comes out, measures, and brings the samples. It's free. Does Thursday at 10 or Friday at 2 work better for both of you?"

Only offer times the client can come. Check their days on Clients and the open times on their calendar. Then get the address.

The other person can't be there, or they're not ready?

"Okay, when would be a good time for us to call you back?"

Near a holiday, try one of these:

"That's perfectly fair. Why don't I call you back in two weeks, after Thanksgiving, and we'll find a time then?"
"Why don't I put something on the calendar for now, and I'll call you two days before to make sure it still works?"

They say yes to a time: book it. If it's a "for now" time, also set a Follow Up Date for two days before and write "tentative" in a note. Otherwise set a Follow Up Date for when they said.

Say it back

Repeat the day, time and address

"Perfect. Thursday at 10 at 123 Oak St. Brian, the owner, will be there. I'll text you so you have it."

Extra questions (optional)

Use these if they want to talk or aren't sure yet. Skip them if they're ready to book. Pick one or two.

  • "Full bathroom, or just the shower?"
  • "When are you hoping to get it done?"
  • "Are you getting a few quotes?"
  • "What made you want to do it now?"

After the call

  1. Book it on the client's calendar. See Book a visit.
  2. Check the Lead Profile and take out any price. See CRM steps.
  3. Move the stage to Booked - Confirmed. About a minute later, the owner gets the Lead Profile by text.
  4. Tell the owner in their group chat. See Sendblue.

Confirm a visit

Some leads book a visit online by themselves. Nobody has talked to them yet, so we don't know if it's real. Your job is to call, make sure they're coming, and change the stage from Booked to Booked - Confirmed. Once you confirm it, it counts as your booking.

How to spot them

  • Your call list says Self-booked: confirm.
  • Their stage says Booked.
  • They have the tag appt-unconfirmed.
  • Their stage isn't Booked? Change it to Booked right away. Everyone who books a visit must be on Booked.
  • You booked them yourself on the phone? Skip this page and go right to Booked - Confirmed.

Steps

1. Look before you call

Open the contact. Find these:

  • The day and time of the visit, on the right side.
  • What they asked for on the form.
  • The city. Is it in the client's area? Check Clients.

You're checking two things: that they're a real person (a lot of bots book online), and that it's a real project. Our clients' jobs start around $10,000, so a new cabinet isn't a fit.

2. Call them

Call from the client's number. Double dial. They hang up fast? Call a 3rd time.

3. What to say

They already said yes by booking, so don't sell. Just get ready for the visit.

Listen to a real one:

Say why you're calling:

"Hey Maria, it's [your name] with Valencia. Saw you booked for Thursday at 10. Got a quick second so we're ready when we come out?"

Ask what they want:

"What were you looking to get done exactly?"

Ask about materials, then who decides:

"Any idea on materials? Tile, slab, acrylic? We bring samples of all the options so you can see them."
"And since we'll be going over all these options, we want to keep it productive. Is there anyone else who helps decide, like a wife or husband, who should be there too?"

Say it back:

"Perfect. Thursday at 10 at 123 Oak St. Brian, the owner, will be there."

Another way to say it

Here's how Leo does these calls. Use whichever feels natural.

"Hey Maria, it's [your name] with Contractor 2.0. You booked for November 21st at 12. You'd be surprised how many bots come through, so I just want to make sure you're a real person."
"I'd also love to learn more about what you've got going on. What kind of bathroom are you thinking of doing?"
"Thanks for sharing. Is 2688 67th Avenue the right address for the visit on the 21st?"
"In case you didn't see the text, you'll be meeting with Esteban, the owner. He'll walk through the project with you and give you material options and numbers on the spot, and he'll have samples. So we're showing everyone the right thing, will anyone else on the title of the house be there too, like your husband?"
"Great. We'll see you then."

4. Check the Lead Profile and leave a note

A few minutes after the call, the Lead Profile on the contact fills in by itself. Read it and fix anything wrong before the next step. The owner gets this exact text. The steps are on CRM steps.

Take out any price you told them. If you talked about money, give a budget range.

5. Change the stage to Booked - Confirmed

  1. On the contact, click Actions.
  2. Click the opportunity (the whole box opens it), or the three dots on it, then View Opportunity details.
  3. Click Stage and pick Booked - Confirmed.
  4. Click Update.

That's it. In about a minute, the CRM stops the confirm calls and texts and sends the owner the Lead Profile by itself. You don't touch the tags.

6. Tell the owner

Post it in the client's group chat, with Confirmed appointment: on the first line. Copy the format, fill it in, and send it. See Sendblue. Now you're done.

Owners treat an unconfirmed visit as a maybe. Your stage change and your post are what tell them to go.

The owner always gets the appointment, even if the lead never answers you. If you couldn't reach them, post it with New appointment (UNCONFIRMED): at the top.

Not every client has a Sendblue chat. The table at the bottom of Sendblue shows where each owner gets their messages.

Check their budget before you book

Some people expect a much lower price, often because of ads that promise prices that aren't real. Seniors get a lot of these. Be honest and kind:

"I'm sorry, that's not realistic for a full bathroom. There's a lot of misinformation out there, so be careful."

If it sounds like the price could be a problem, check before you book:

"Just so you know, this starts at around $15,000. Is that okay?"

Texting

Your job is calling. The workflows already text leads for us, so don't text unless you have a reason. When you do text, write like a normal person: one or two short lines and one question.

Most texts aren't from a person

Most of what you see in Conversations was sent by the CRM: the welcome text, appointment reminders, and the text that goes out about 5 minutes after a missed call from your list. Reminder texts to the owners show up there too. Leave those alone. You only step in when a lead writes back.

Only text when there's a reason

No reason on this list? Don't text. Call instead.

  • They asked you to text them.
  • They texted you first.
  • You were talking and they dropped off, and now you can't reach them.
  • You have another real reason, like you need one thing from them to get the visit ready.

Never send a text just to check in or because they didn't answer. The workflow already does that. And try to get them on a call: booking by text is the last resort.

Following up

Don't send

Hi Seema, this is [your name] with Valencia Construction Pros. Just wanted to follow up and remind you to give us a call when you have a moment so we can schedule your free, no-obligation estimate for your new laundry room with bathroom remodeling project. Hey Seema, it's [your name]. Still want that free estimate for the laundry room?

Getting the address

Don't send

Thanks for letting me know, Phil! Since it's a big rush, we'll make sure to prioritize your estimate. Can you send me the address where the work will be done? That way I can get you on the calendar ASAP.

Send

Got it Phil, we'll get you in fast. What's the address?

Texts that work, when you have a reason

Phil you are harder to reach than the president!

Are you in a rush to get this done or just looking around?

Hey Phil saw you booked for tomorrow. Can you let me know a good time to reach you before we come out?

Does Thursday at 10 or Friday at 2 work better?

Write like you're texting a friend. Short is better, and a typo is fine. Some of the CRM's best texts are just their name and a question mark, like "Seema?"

CRM steps

The CRM sends texts and reminders by itself. Your part is calling, booking, notes, the Lead Profile and the stage.

1. Open Manual Actions

Click Conversations, then Manual Actions. This is your call list. Call new leads first, then self-booked leads, then reschedules, then everyone else.

It shows 20 calls a page. Click Next at the bottom to see the rest. Your list is done for the day when it's empty.

To see one group at a time, use Select Workflow at the top. For example, pick New HSD Masonry Facebook Lead to see only HSD's new leads.

2. Work your list one of two ways

Either way, every call has to come off your list when you're done with it. That's how the CRM knows you called, so it can move on to its next step.

The fast way: Let's Start

  1. Pick one client's list in Select Workflow, like New HSD Masonry Facebook Lead.
  2. Set the dialer to that client's number first (step 3). Let's Start calls from whatever number is set.
  3. Click Let's Start. It opens the contact and dials right away, so read their info while it rings.
  4. No answer? Click Pause, then call again for the double dial. Then click Done.
  5. The call comes off your list by itself, and the next one starts.

One at a time

  1. Click the lead's name in the list. Read who they are: what they asked for, their tags, and past calls and texts.
  2. Open the dialer, pick the client's number (step 3), and double dial.
  3. Go back to Manual Actions and click the trash can on that lead's row. If you skip this, the CRM doesn't know you called.

Only use the trash can in Manual Actions. The trash can next to their name on the contact page deletes the whole contact. Never click that one.

If a box asks "Call Anyway?" because it's late for them, press Cancel.

3. Call from the client's number

The dialer doesn't switch numbers for you, so check it before every call. Open the dialer, click Change next to "Calling From," type the client's name, and pick the number that says USE THIS. The numbers are on the Clients page.

Never use a number marked OLD SPAM or CLIENT TEXTS ONLY. Phones flag OLD SPAM numbers as spam, so fewer people pick up.

4. New lead didn't answer: add lead nurturing

Do this after the double dial and the voicemail, every time. Without it, the CRM can forget the lead.

  1. On the contact, click the + next to Tags.
  2. Type lead nurturing and click it.
  3. Click Apply.

Right away, an automatic text should show up in their conversation. They'll come back to your list about 4 hours later.

Don't see the text? Refresh the page. Still nothing? Add the tag again, or text Leo.

Leads already getting our texts (Nurture in your list) don't need it. Never add it to someone who has a visit booked.

5. Write notes while you talk

In the notes on the contact, write what they want, materials, who'll be there, their timing, and anything a robot would miss. For example: "Needs a walk-in shower because she just started using a wheelchair." Owners love knowing why.

The CRM also records the call, writes a summary, and fills in the address under All fields by itself. Your notes catch what it misses.

Don't leave notes on people who haven't answered.

6. Book the visit

On the contact, click the calendar icon, then Add Appointment. Every step is on the Book a visit page.

The box opens on the wrong client. Every time, pick the client's calendar, the client's name as team member, and the time zone.

7. Check the Lead Profile

On the contact, click All fields and search Lead Profile. It fills in by itself a few minutes after a call. Still empty, or you booked by text? Write it yourself.

The owner gets this exact text when you set Booked - Confirmed, so fix it first:

  • Name, Phone and Address stay at the top.
  • What they want done, in their words.
  • Who will be there and the best way to reach them.
  • Plain text. Keep it short.

Never write a price you told them. Give a budget range instead, like "Budget: around $18K to $28K."

On the lead's contact, click the calendar icon on the right, then Add Appointment.

2. Pick the client's calendar

The box usually opens on the wrong client. Click Calendar, type the client's name, and click their calendar.

Not sure which client? Look at the lead's tags. Calendar names are on the Clients page.

If you pick the wrong calendar, the lead gets texts from the wrong company and the wrong client gets the visit.

3. Pick the client's name as team member

Click Team member and pick the client's name, like Watkinson Construction. Each client has its own name in this list. It keeps that client's visits from landing on top of each other.
