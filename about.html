About

About Build Signals

Build Signals (also written BuildSignals) books in-home appointments for bathroom remodelers across the United States. Leo Melaye founded the company in 2016. We're based in Davis, California, and work with remodelers nationwide.

What we do

  • We write and run Facebook and Instagram ads in your service area.
  • We call every new lead in under 5 minutes and qualify them: homeowner, real project, budget.
  • We book in-home appointments on your calendar and confirm them before you drive out.
  • You see every lead, appointment and closed job in a live dashboard.

Your leads are yours. We never share or resell them, and we work with one remodeler per area.

How you pay

Pay per sat appointment or per close. You choose: a price per sat appointment, set for your market on our call, or an agreed percentage of the jobs you close. No long-term contracts. You pay for the ads directly in your own ad account, which you own.

Who's behind it

Leo Melaye founded Build Signals in 2016 and runs it with a small team in Davis, California.
