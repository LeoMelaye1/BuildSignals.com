About Build Signals

Build Signals (also written BuildSignals) books in-home appointments for contractors and home improvement companies across the United States: remodelers, roofers and every other trade that sells in the home. Leo Melaye founded the company in 2016. We're based in Davis, California, and work with companies nationwide.

What we do

  • We write and run Facebook and Instagram ads in your service area, in your own ad account.
  • We call every new lead in under 5 minutes and qualify them: homeowner, real project, budget.
  • We book in-home appointments on your calendar and confirm them before you drive out.
  • You see every lead, appointment and closed job in a live dashboard.

Your leads are yours. We never share or resell them, and we work with one company per trade in each area.

How you pay

  • $1 today puts your card on file.
  • Your first 30 days: no retainer. You pay $100 per shown appointment, charged once a week.
  • After day 30: $1,000 a month plus $100 per shown appointment.
  • Your ad budget goes straight to Meta from your own ad account, which you own. The minimum is $75 a day.

A shown appointment means the homeowner is there and meets your rep at the booked time. No-shows and cancellations cost you nothing. No long-term contract.
